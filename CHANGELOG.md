v1.0.0
- First stable release. All features are unchanged from v0.1.0; this version marks the mod's first public release on CurseForge and Modrinth.

v0.1.0
- Initial release for Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge 1.21.1.
- Factory blocks and Tier Installer items now show a "Lines: N" tooltip, so you can tell a machine's tier without placing it.
- Applied Energistics 2 terminal: added an optional toggle to group and sort Factories, Tier Installers, Fluid/Chemical Tanks, and Energy Cubes by tier. The setting is remembered between play sessions.
- Jade: looking at a Factory, Tank, Energy Cube, Cable, Pipe, Tube, or Transporter now shows its capacity, rate, or Lines count in the tooltip.
- Jade: sneaking while holding a matching Tier Installer or Alloy previews the values the block would have after the upgrade.
- Known limitation: the upgrade preview is not shown when using a MekanismExtras Alloy near an EvolvedMekanismExtras transmitter (the upgrade itself still works in-game).
